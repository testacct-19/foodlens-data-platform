# FoodLens

## Vision

FoodLens helps households reduce food waste by tracking grocery inventory and generating insights.

## Problem Statement

Users forget what groceries they already own.

Food expires.

Money is wasted.

Shopping becomes inefficient.

## Goals

- Track grocery inventory
- Monitor expiry dates
- Reduce food waste
- Generate shopping insights
- Provide analytics dashboard

## Target Users

Individuals

Small families

Students living away from home

Young professionals

## Success Metrics

Reduce duplicate purchases

Reduce food waste

Track grocery spending

Improve inventory visibilitys
